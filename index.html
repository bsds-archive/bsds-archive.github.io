Why BSDS

Four pillars, one programme.

Most data science degrees bolt a few statistics courses onto a computer science curriculum. BSDS does the opposite — it starts from ISI's seventy-year foundation in statistical theory, and builds outward into the mathematics, probability and economics that make data actually mean something. You don't just learn to run a model. You learn why it works, when it breaks, and what to do next.

Statistical Rigor

A curriculum built on ISI's decades of statistical pedigree — theory first, applications close behind.

Mathematical Foundations

Real analysis, linear algebra and calculus woven through every semester, not bolted on at the end.

Probabilistic Thinking

From first principles to stochastic processes — probability as a way of reasoning, not just a subject to pass.

Electives

Markets, incentives and behaviour — because data science doesn't exist in a vacuum.
